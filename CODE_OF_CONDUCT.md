# Community conduct

Participate constructively and discuss the work rather than attacking people.

Expected behaviour includes:

- giving technically specific, evidence-backed feedback;
- respecting privacy and security boundaries;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, irrelevant, or privacy-violating material and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
