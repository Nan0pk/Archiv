# S12 — Graph person becomes candidate_mention; remove hardcoded confidences

**Phase B** (Stop the tool overclaiming) · depends on: S11

## Capability declaration


## What changes in this step

The current graph builder treats every two-capitalized-word regex match that survives an
English denylist as a `person`. That is not evidence of a person. S12 keeps the match only
as a `candidate_mention`, so phrases such as `New Delhi`, `Machine Learning`, `Legal
Counsel`, and `Terracotta Vase` cannot be reported as people merely because they fit the
regex. A later step may classify candidates with stronger evidence.

Confirmed face identities remain people: S12 does not weaken explicit user confirmation
or reinterpret evidence already strong enough to establish identity.

The graph also stores fixed confidence values such as `0.90`, `0.85`, `0.80`, and `0.75`
and the CLI renders some of them as percentages. Those numbers were never measured. S12
must remove numeric confidence from user-visible graph output unless the producing path
can point to a real measurement. Internal relationship/status representation may remain
only where it does not turn an invented number into a user claim; this step does not
invent replacement scores.

## Why this step exists

Person extraction is the regex `\b([A-Z][a-z]+ [A-Z][a-z]+)\b` filtered by a
hand-maintained English denylist. Reproduced on a five-line minutes fixture: three of ten
`person` nodes were people. The other seven were `The Working` (a truncation of "The
Working Group"), `Deep Learning`, `Machine Learning`, `Legal Counsel`, `Agenda Item`,
`New Delhi` — and `Terracotta Vase`, inherited from the former face detector.

`archiv graph entity "New Delhi"` prints `Entity Profile: New Delhi (person)` with a
genuine citation and a "75.0%" co-occurrence confidence. The citation is real and
checkable — that part of the design works. What overclaims is the type and the number.

The denylist contains `new york`, `hong kong` and `los angeles` but not `new delhi`.
There is no version of that list that ends, which is why S27 replaces the approach rather
than extending the list.

## Acceptance

The queue's existing targets are confirmed unchanged:

```
tests/test_entity_graph_precision.py::test_known_false_positives_are_not_typed_person
tests/test_entity_graph_precision.py::test_no_unmeasured_confidence_value_reaches_user_output
```

The first test must exercise generated text containing known false positives and prove
that none is typed `person`. It should also protect the positive boundary: an explicitly
confirmed face identity remains a person.

The second test must exercise the human-readable and machine-readable graph surfaces that
can expose relationship confidence and prove that no fixed, unmeasured numeric confidence
is reported. Removing only the percent sign or changing the displayed number does not
satisfy it.

## Must not change

- Do not extend the English denylist as the solution.
- Do not introduce a model, network call, new dependency, or guessed replacement score.
- Do not alter originals or canonical document evidence.
- Do not remove source citations merely to remove confidence values.
- Do not implement S27's later entity classifier in this step.
