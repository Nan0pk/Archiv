## Why this step exists

The blocker here is lawful fixtures, not code. `docs/security/threat-model.md`
forbids fixtures containing third-party or user documents, and face fixtures are
photographs of identifiable people. LFW, CelebA and similar academic sets are not
redistributable under this policy.

Three lawful routes, best first: synthetic rendered heads from a permissively-licensed 3D
model — lawful, redistributable, no real person, and parameterisable across pose and
lighting; the negative fixtures from S11, which need no licence at all; and reporting
YuNet's published benchmark figures **by citation** rather than shipping anyone's
images.
