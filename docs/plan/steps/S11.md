## Why this step exists

The face detector is `_is_skin_tone(r, g, b)` plus connected components on a
128x128 downsample, filtered by aspect ratio and bounding-box fill. Reproduced: a tan
ellipse on white was filed as `Person 1`, and the attribution layer proposed the name
**"Terracotta Vase (52% conf)"** from the filename. The confidence is
`0.50 + fill_ratio * 0.45` — arithmetic on blob solidity, not a detection probability.

Until S24 lands a real detector, ship clustering only and propose no names at all. That
is the fallback `docs/capability-expansion-plan.md` itself specified. The privacy
scaffolding around it is good and stays: opt-in gating, `Unconfirmed` status, first-class
erasure.

The negative fixture suite written here is the test that would have caught this, and it
costs nothing: a vase, a wooden table, a sunset, a brick wall. It is also reused as
acceptance for S24.
