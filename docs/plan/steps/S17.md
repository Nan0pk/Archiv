# S17 — PPTX: speaker notes, tables, charts, grouped shapes, placeholder roles, hidden slides

**Phase C** (Recover the content being silently dropped) · depends on: S16

## Why this step exists

Reproduced: a slide containing a title, a 3x2 table with six populated cells,
and a speaker note yielded **one segment** — the title. Roughly seven eighths of the
slide's information was dropped.

Two causes. `getattr(shape, "text", "")` returns `""` for a `GraphicFrame`, which is what
a table is, so the whole table is skipped silently. And speaker notes
(`slide.notes_slide`) are never read at all — frequently the densest text in a deck.
Grouped shapes are also not recursed, so anything grouped is invisible.

The stub for this step said `normalize_odf.py:405` already handles `presentation:notes`
for the OpenDocument equivalent. That was wrong: line 405 **skips** OpenDocument speaker
notes, and the opendocument-presentation row of `docs/format-compatibility.json` says so
in its `known_limits`. There is no existing notes reader to copy. Bringing OpenDocument
presentations level with this step belongs to S18, not here.

## Capability declaration

```yaml

purpose: >
  Index the text of every shape on every slide, including shapes inside groups, the
  cells of slide tables, chart titles, axis titles and series names, and each slide's
  speaker notes; record the role of each placeholder (title, subtitle, body and so on);
  and mark every segment from a hidden slide or a hidden shape, so it can be searched
  and cited without hiding where it came from.
input_schema: a canonical .pptx original that python-pptx can open
output_schema: >
  NormalizedDocument with segments and one table per slide table. Locator shapes are
  listed for the office-presentation family in docs/format-compatibility.json.
reads: the original's package parts (presentation, slides, notes slides, charts and
  relationships) through python-pptx; chart parts are re-read with the standard library
writes: derived/** only, through the existing normalization path
forbidden_paths: objects/** (originals are never modified), config/**, runs/**
network_policy: none; nothing a slide refers to is fetched
timeout_policy: bounded by the ingestion size limit and by two ceilings in
  src/archiv/ingestion/normalize_office.py, all checked before any original is stored.
  MAX_XML_NODES counts every element this reader walks, across the whole presentation,
  counted again each time the same part is reached through another reference, so a
  chart or notes page referenced many times cannot multiply the work. MAX_TABLE_CELLS

  checked for DTD and entity declarations, refused outright, before python-pptx parses

evidence_produced: tests/test_ooxml_recovery.py, and the office-presentation row of
  docs/format-compatibility.json checked against live ingestion by
  tests/test_format_matrix.py
validator: pytest acceptance targets below
```

## Decisions made in this step

- **Shape numbering is unchanged.** `shape` is still the 1-based position among the
  slide's top-level shapes, counting shapes with no text, so a citation's "slide 2,
  shape 3" names the same shape it did under the old reader. Text of an ordinary shape
  is exactly what `python-pptx` gives for `shape.text`, as before.
- **A shape inside a group** keeps its group's top-level `shape` number and adds
  `grouped_shape`: its 1-based position inside the group, as text, with a dot for each
  level of nesting (`"2"`, or `"2.1"` for the first shape inside the group's second
  shape). Groups nested more than 32 deep refuse the presentation rather than recurse
  without bound.
- **Slide tables** yield one segment per non-empty cell, located by `slide`, `shape`,
  `row` and `column` (both 1-based), matching the Word reader. A cell covered by a merge
  carries no text of its own and yields nothing. The slide also gets a
  `NormalizedTable`, located by `slide` and `shape`.
- **Charts** yield one segment per chart, located by `slide`, `shape` and `chart` = its
  1-based position among that slide's charts: the title, then axis titles, then series
  names, one per line. This is the same text, read by the same code, as the Excel
  reader's chart segments; that code moves into a shared module. A chart whose part is
  missing from the package is skipped, not guessed.
- **Placeholder roles.** A placeholder shape's locator carries `placeholder`, in plain
  words: `title` (for both title and centred title), `subtitle`, `body`, `content`,
  `date`, `footer`, `header`, `slide number`, `chart`, `table`, `picture`, `diagram`,
  `media`, `clip art` or `slide image`. A type this list does not know is kept as
  written in the file.
- **Speaker notes** yield one segment per text-bearing shape on the slide's notes page,
  located by `slide` and `speaker_notes` = 1, 2 and so on in order. The notes page's
  slide image, slide number, date, header and footer placeholders are not notes and are
  skipped. A slide with no notes page is not given one.
- **Hidden content is indexed and marked, not withheld**, as for Excel. Every segment
  from a slide saved with `show="0"` carries `hidden` = `slide`, speaker notes included.
  A shape saved with `hidden="1"`, or anything inside a hidden group, carries
  `hidden` = `shape`. When both apply, `slide` is recorded. Metadata counts
  `hidden_slides`.
- The `pptx` extractor version moves from 1 to 2.

## Not in this step

Recorded in the office-presentation `known_limits`: hyperlink targets, picture and
shape alternative text, SmartArt diagram text, embedded objects, the values and category
labels a chart plots, comments, animation and transition order, and speaker-notes timing.
Presentations added before this change keep their older derivative until
`archiv rebuild-derived` is run for them; nothing rebuilds them automatically. The
OpenDocument presentation reader still skips speaker notes (S18).
