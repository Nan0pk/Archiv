## Why this step exists

Video is currently rejected at the door, so a video in a corpus is invisible
rather than merely unreadable.

Treat a video as a container of three evidence streams rather than as a new format with a
new pipeline. This step does the first two: container metadata (creation time, device,
duration, codec, and GPS through the existing privacy-protected handling), and extracting
the audio track into the S20 transcription path. Most of what is findable in a video is
in its audio, which is why this comes before keyframes.

An audio-only `.mp4` should route to the S19 audio path, not the video path.
