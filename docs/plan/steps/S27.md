## Why this step exists

Native `.inp` Urdu ingestion and `ara`/`urd` OCR are headline capabilities, but
the graph's extractor is `\b([A-Z][a-z]+ [A-Z][a-z]+)\b` — ASCII-only, so no Urdu or
Arabic name can ever be extracted from any of it. Adding this later means re-deriving
every edge, which is why it gates promoting the graph past experimental rather than
following it.

Replace the character-class regex and the unbounded English denylist with Unicode
script-aware extraction covering Arabic script alongside Latin. Then promote
`candidate_mention` back to `person` only where a measured precision threshold is met, per
script — and keep the S12 regression list as a permanent test.
