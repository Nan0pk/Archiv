## Why this step exists

No accuracy claim ships without a number. Generate lawful synthetic speech
fixtures in English, Arabic and Urdu — mirroring how `_ocr_benchmark_corpus.py` already
builds its multilingual corpus — and report word error rate per language, with runtime,
memory, and the exact model hash.

`docs/ocr-benchmark-report.md` is the template, including its disclaimer discipline: it
opens by stating what the measurement is *not* evidence for.
