## Why this step exists

Name candidates come only from sources a user can open and check: EXIF
`Artist`, `XPAuthor`, `CameraOwnerName`; IPTC caption and byline; XMP `dc:creator`; or a
document segment that names the image, which is already retrievable through FTS and
already the shape the graph's `GraphCitation` uses.

Every candidate ships with its citation. No citation, no candidate. Confidence is either
a measured detector score or absent — never an arithmetic restatement of geometry.

Filename-derived names were removed in S11 and do not come back. That is what produced
"Terracotta Vase".

Erasure must stay first-class and must also remove the graph edges derived from a
cluster, not just the cluster itself.
